About the App

WHO Standard Health Tracker helps users log and understand evidence-based body measurements and wellness indicators including BMI, Blood Pressure tracking, Water Intake, Body Surface Area (BSA), Waist-to-Hip Ratio (WHR), and general wellness guidance.
