# Health Metrics Tracker — ASO and launch plan

Status: prepared for review; nothing in this document publishes the app.

Research date: 2 September 2026. Play Store pages and policy pages can change, so recheck the console immediately before submission.

## Positioning

Health Metrics Tracker should be presented as a calm personal wellness companion: quick daily logging, understandable calculators, and explainable trends. It is not a medical device, diagnostic service or replacement for professional care. The strongest defensible angle is the combination of broad calculator coverage, local-first tracking, clear limitations and optional connections rather than a promise of clinical accuracy.

## Competitor scan

The current Play Store search surface is crowded around the same high-intent phrases. The following listings were reviewed on the research date:

| Listing | Visible positioning | Opportunity for Health Metrics Tracker |
| --- | --- | --- |

| [Ideal Weight - BMI Calculator](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=mmapps.bmi.calculator) | Long-running weight wheel, BMI and multi-period charts; listing shows 5M+ downloads, ads and in-app purchases. | Make methods, limitations and privacy controls visible before and after a result. |
| [Better Weight](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.betterweightapp) | Simple weight/BMI tracker with 7-day low and charts; listing shows 1M+ downloads and in-app purchases. | Pair the quick weigh-in with water, steps, blood-pressure logs and explainable weekly insights. |
| [BMI Calculator: Weight Tracker (SoloCode)](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.bmi.calculator.health.weight.tracker.app) | Keyword-rich all-in-one BMI, body-fat, BMR, TDEE, ideal-weight and water listing; claims offline use and no subscription. | Avoid unsupported “complete/accurate/science-backed” claims and lead with trust, scope and evidence. |
| [BMI Calc: BRI, Weight, No Ads](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.flx_apps.bmicalculator) | Privacy-focused calculator with an optional one-time unlock. | Validate that privacy and honest interpretation can be a meaningful differentiator without a fake paywall. |

These listings show demand for BMI, weight, calorie, water and trend language, but public Play pages do not provide reliable keyword-volume or conversion data. Treat this as directional demand/competition research; use Play Console acquisition reports, store-listing experiments and search-query data after launch before changing the title.

## Store metadata draft

### App name

`Health Metrics Tracker` (22 characters; keep the requested product name and the existing application ID `com.healthmetrics.tracker`).

### Short description

`Track weight, water, steps and wellness insights with helpful calculators`

This is 73 characters and describes the primary value without a ranking, price, urgency, medical or unsupported accuracy claim.

### Full description

Health Metrics Tracker is a calm personal wellness companion for recording the information you choose to track and understanding it over time.

Use quick check-ins for:

- Weight and goal progress
- Water and hydration habits
- Optional food and calorie notes
- Home blood-pressure reading history
- Steps from Health Connect when you choose to connect it

Explore informational calculators for BMI, basal metabolic rate, daily calorie estimates, ideal weight, water intake, waist ratios, body surface area, blood-pressure categories, metabolic markers and heart-rate zones. Each tool explains its inputs, method, interpretation and limitations. Results are estimates for general wellness education, not diagnoses or treatment plans.

Build a useful routine without pressure:

- Review local history and edit or remove entries
- Compare recent patterns with deterministic, explainable insights
- Read a weekly wellness summary based on the check-ins you recorded
- Choose gentle reminders only when they help
- Add an Android home-screen widget for selected progress information

The optional AI Wellness Assistant can answer general wellness questions. It is not a doctor. App context is off unless you explicitly enable it, and the assistant is designed not to diagnose, prescribe or recommend medication changes.

Your choices stay in your hands:

- Core tracking and calculators work without an account
- Health Connect access is optional, read-only and requested per feature
- Most wellness records remain in the app’s private on-device storage by default
- You choose what to include when exporting or sharing a report
- Privacy Policy, Terms and data deletion controls are available in the app

Health Metrics Tracker is for informational wellness use. It is not a medical device and does not replace a qualified healthcare professional. If you have a health concern, seek advice from an appropriate professional. Do not use an app estimate to make an urgent or medication decision.

### Feature bullets for graphics and web copy

- A clear daily wellness snapshot
- Quick weight, water and habit check-ins
- Informational calculators with method and limits
- Explainable trends without diagnosis or blame
- Optional Health Connect steps and weight access
- Share only the report sections you choose
- Optional AI wellness conversations with consent-based context

Avoid putting raw health values, “WHO-standard” claims, emergency language, ranking claims, price promotions or medical promises in listing artwork.

## Keyword strategy

Use natural language in the title, short description and first paragraph. Do not repeat terms in a list or hide keywords in metadata.

| Priority | Phrase | Competition / intent | Planned use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | health tracker | High intent, very crowded | First paragraph and feature bullets |
| 2 | wellness tracker | High intent, less clinical framing | Positioning and description |
| 3 | weight tracker | High intent, crowded | Tracking section and screenshot 2 |
| 4 | BMI calculator | Very crowded, strong calculator intent | Calculator section and screenshot 4 |
| 5 | water tracker | Clear habit intent | Tracking section and screenshot 3 |
| 6 | health metrics | Lower-volume but distinctive | Product name/description language |
| 7 | health calculator | Crowded, broad | Calculator paragraph only |
| 8 | step tracker | Crowded and dependent on Health Connect | Optional connection paragraph |
| 9 | blood pressure log | Sensitive, high trust/policy scrutiny | Describe the actual log; do not use as a diagnosis claim |

Do not target “medical device”, “diagnosis”, “cure”, “emergency”, “doctor replacement”, “guaranteed accurate”, “WHO standard” or competitor names. Those terms are misleading or create policy and trust risk.

## Category and policy

Recommend the Play category **Health & Fitness**, not Medical: the app supports personal wellness tracking and general calculators rather than clinical care. Google’s category guidance describes Health and Fitness as personal fitness, workout tracking, diet/nutrition tips, and health and safety. Select no more than five tags and choose only tags that are plainly supported by the initial experience.

Complete the Health apps declaration, Data safety form, privacy-policy URL, content rating and Health Connect disclosures in Play Console. Health Connect is personal and sensitive data: list only the read permissions actually used, explain their user-facing purpose and keep consent/revocation paths visible. Confirm the current developer-account requirements before submission.

## Screenshot plan

Prepare phone screenshots with synthetic demo data, consistent blue-green branding, large accessible text and no personal information. Use short overlays rather than repeating the full description:

1. Home — “See today’s wellness snapshot”
2. Weight — “Log a weigh-in in seconds”
3. Water — “Build a hydration rhythm”
4. Calculators — “Understand the method and limits”
5. Insights — “Spot changes without diagnosis”
6. Health Connect — “Connect steps only when you choose”
7. Weekly report — “Review your week and share selected sections”
8. AI assistant — “Ask general wellness questions — context is optional”

Use the first two screenshots to explain the product in search previews. Do not show a concerning reading, medication suggestion, fake testimonial, before/after body claim or “perfect score.”

## Feature graphic and icon concept

- Feature graphic: 1024×500 JPEG/24-bit PNG, an abstract blue-green path connecting small water, weight and trend shapes; no hospital cross, body silhouette, ranking badge, price or dense text. Keep the central area quiet so the icon and listing title remain distinct.
- App icon: retain a simple geometric wellness mark with a single focal shape and high contrast; no words, numbers, medical cross or promotional badge. Export a 512×512 32-bit PNG with alpha under 1 MB.
- Verify light/dark contrast, color-blind readability and legibility at launcher size before upload.

## Onboarding copy

1. **Make the numbers useful** — “Choose a few metrics to record. You can start with one check-in and add more later.”
2. **Understand, don’t diagnose** — “Calculators and insights explain patterns and limits. They are informational, not medical advice.”
3. **Stay in control** — “Health Connect, reminders, AI context and sharing are optional. Decide what this app can access.”

## Promotional copy

Short web/social variant: “A calmer way to track weight, water, steps and everyday wellness patterns — with calculators that show their method and limits.”

Long variant: “Health Metrics Tracker brings daily check-ins, informational health calculators, explainable trends and optional Health Connect data into one respectful Android companion. Your routine is yours: choose what to record, what to connect and what to share.”

## Release notes draft — 1.0.0

“Meet Health Metrics Tracker: quick weight, water, steps and wellness check-ins, informational calculators with clear methods and limits, optional Health Connect connections, explainable insights and shareable weekly summaries. Built for personal wellness—not diagnosis.”

## Final pre-publish checklist

- Verify the title and short/full description limits in Play Console (30/80/4,000 characters respectively).
- Upload a 512×512 icon, a 1024×500 feature graphic and at least two phone screenshots; add alt text and test previews.
- Complete Health apps, Data safety, content rating and Health Connect declarations.
- Host the final privacy policy and terms at stable public URLs and verify links from a clean browser.
- Run a closed test with representative devices, denied permissions, no network, empty data and dark mode.
- Review all screenshots, logs, notifications and exports for personal data before capture.
- Confirm support email, developer identity, target audience, account type, signing, crash reporting and release notes.
- Run the release AAB checks and use Play’s pre-launch report before any production rollout.
