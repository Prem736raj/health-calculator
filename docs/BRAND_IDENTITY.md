# Health Metrics Tracker — brand identity

## Position

**Health Metrics Tracker** is a calm companion for personal wellness: simple daily check-ins, explainable estimates and trends people control. It is not a medical device, a diagnostic service or a substitute for professional care.

The product should feel reassuring and useful—not clinical, competitive or demanding. A missed day is neutral; a logged pattern is information, not a grade.

## Voice

- Say **track**, **notice**, **estimate**, **trend**, **may** and **consider**.
- Lead with the user's control: optional connections, local records and selected sharing.
- Say **informational wellness use** when a health boundary matters.
- Do not say or imply diagnosis, medical certainty, guaranteed outcomes, treatment or pressure to keep a streak.

## Visual signature

- Use the warm canvas and surface roles from the Compose theme; reserve the deep wellness hero treatment for the daily score, not every card.
- Use a display face for calm section moments, tabular measurement text for metrics and the body face for explanations.
- Treat the outlined heart as a wellness mark, not a clinical emblem. Keep icons outline-led and use feature accents only to group related information.
- Keep three component tiers distinct: a layered hero for the daily overview, compact metric tiles for readings, and low-emphasis navigation rows for destinations.
