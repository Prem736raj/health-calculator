Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Health Metrics Tracker ("we", "our", or "the app") collects, uses, and protects your information.

Summary: Your personal health metrics (such as BMI, Blood Pressure, WHR, Water Intake, and BSA) are stored locally on your device by default. We do not sell your personal data or share your health records with third-party advertisers.

1. Information We Collect

  • Health & Wellness Data: Height, weight, blood pressure measurements, waist/hip circumferences, and daily hydration logs that you choose to input. This data is stored locally in the app-private Room database on your device. The app does not claim database-level encryption.
  • Health Connect Data (Optional): If you grant permission, the app requests read-only access to step records (android.permission.health.READ_STEPS) to compute your daily activity metrics on your device. We do not transfer your Health Connect data to third-party servers.
  • AI Wellness Assistant Queries: Text messages you send to the AI Wellness Assistant are processed via Google Firebase AI Logic / Gemini to generate wellness responses. Do not submit passwords or sensitive financial information in chat.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To calculate evidence-based body and wellness indices (BMI, Body Surface Area, WHR, etc.).
  • To track your hydration, streak milestones, and wellness habits over time.
  • To provide general wellness educational information via AI when requested.

3. Data Storage & Security

Core health records remain in the application's private Android sandbox on your device. Automatic Android backups are disabled (android:allowBackup="false"). When you choose to export a report or data file, the selected content is handed to the Android share/files system and may be stored by the destination you choose. The app does not currently offer automatic cloud backup or cross-device restore.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Google Firebase AI Logic: Processes AI chat queries to generate educational wellness information under Google Cloud's security standards.
  • Firebase App Check / Play Integrity: Verifies application authenticity to prevent abuse.

5. Medical Disclaimer

Health Metrics Tracker is an educational wellness tool and is not a medical device. Calculations and AI suggestions are estimates and do not constitute medical diagnosis, treatment, or prescription.

6. Data Deletion & Rights

You can delete all stored data at any time directly within the app under Settings > Data Management > Clear All Data, or by uninstalling the application from your device.
